## Content and technology combined

I'm a content strategist with a need to understand how people use technology to communicate. My interest in the field started at Ithaca College, where I studied Journalism and spent time rebuilding the campus radio station's website — my first real lesson in what it means to design for an audience, not just yourself.

Since then I've worked at the intersection of content, technology, and user experience. I care about information architecture, the way structure shapes understanding, and how the tools we use to build the web keep changing underneath us. After years of publishing on Medium, I built this site to learn modern web development firsthand and to have a place to write that I actually own.

I'm currently exploring what it looks like to build with AI — this blog itself was put together with Claude's help. When I'm not writing here, I'm probably thinking about how people find what they're looking for online (and why so many sites make that needlessly hard).
