Since everyone's talking about Medium's clapping, I thought I'd share my method for doling them out.

The way I see it, anytime I read a good line in a Medium story, I clap. Similarly to if I were listening to someone speak, good ideas and thoughts get claps. If I am bored or don't agree, I'm not clapping. For some stories, this means I'm clapping almost every line or paragraph. For others, it means I just clap once at the end to show appreciation for writing down their thoughts, but I didn't particularly like it. But hey, I'm still here, so that's worth something.

The X factor is the end. Like any presentation, I might throw a bunch of extra claps on the end if I think the overall piece was exceptionally good. If it wasn't, I'll just clap politely (once) and move on.

Some of what I have been reading suggests claps should be available in chunks, like bursts of 5 or on a slider. The problem for me with those is that it feels like it becomes a rating system out of 50, and not an organic way of showing praise. If I read a story that was very good, I'll feel pressured to give it a 50 (or near 50). I happen to like the friction of time to keep the scores from over-inflating, especially if Medium really does want to tie monetization to claps.
