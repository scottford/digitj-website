Analytics supplies you with a vast amount of data, but it can be difficult to know what to do with all of that information. Google Analytics provides some basic reports and general insights, but they’re too broad and unfocused to provide you the potential deeper insights. Rather than reporting on general user numbers and high-level findings, implement a plan that is customized to your business and starts to uncover what’s most important to your users.
