Our designers and UX specialists attended Figma’s [Config 2025](https://config.figma.com/san-francisco/) conference and brought back insights and ideas for how to improve the design process, collaborate more effectively, and create great products. A theme that runs through each point is a need for clear communication and common ground from which everyone can work together to create something everyone agrees is high quality.
